Chicken Biryani

A delicious and spicy Pakistani rice dish made with aromatic spices and tender chicken.

👤 Author

RecipeShare User

📅 Date

19 September 2026

⏱ Cooking Time

60 Minutes

🍽 Category

Pakistani

Ingredients

  • 500g Chicken
  • 2 Cups Rice
  • 2 Onions
  • 2 Tomatoes
  • Biryani Masala
  • Salt
  • Cooking Oil

Instructions

  1. Wash and soak the rice.
  2. Cook the chicken with spices.
  3. Boil the rice separately.
  4. Layer the rice and chicken.
  5. Cook on low heat for 15 minutes.
  6. Serve hot.
